## Dog Park Weather Rating, like you're five

Is it a good time to take the dog out? This little box tells you.

It looks at the weather: is it raining, is it windy, is it too hot, is it too cold, is it dark? Then it gives one answer with paws. Five paws is great. One paw is "maybe stay home".

Smart people who run a website can put this box on their pages and tell it which park to check. They can even change the words, so it can speak Greek or any other language.
